## BIDEA

Hallo. This is Liam, in for Bear. ClearAnswer starts with what someone wrote: a research claim. Its architecture connects extracted claims to data tools, evidence, and reviewer decisions. That is a useful way to turn a fluent report into questions we can actually check.

## BDEFS

Three terms. Claim means a statement that needs supporting evidence. Trace means a record of the computations performed. Uncertainty means what remains unresolved or unsupported.

## B01

Take a claim, identify the computation it needs, then inspect the evidence. ClearAnswer separates the agent, semantic, and data layers. That structure makes it possible to distinguish a plausible sentence from a checked numerical result.
