## BIDEA

Hallo. This is Liam, in for Bear. SNS Cohort Control Tower makes the effect of a data change part of the conversation. It brings together before-and-after analysis, evidence, and repair simulations. That gives reviewers something more useful than a bare warning.

## BDEFS

Three terms. Fingerprint means a digest of specific file contents. Impact means how an analysis changes. Simulation means a proposed outcome under stated assumptions.

## B01

Follow the chain from the earlier release to the change and then the new result. SNS records analysis dependencies and evidence. That structure helps a researcher investigate why an answer moved.

## B02

This is the actual SNS interface, run locally with invented data. The review exercised its engine, API, and browser. The results view makes the comparison inspectable, with evidence connected to the affected analysis.

## B03

The export workflow is another useful part of the project. It gives the reviewer a concrete artifact to inspect. The next development opportunity is to keep the authority behind every proposed data change just as clear as the change itself.

## B10

The output includes file-content fingerprints and distinguishes analysis failures from ordinary comparison. It also represents repair simulations. Keep those simulations clearly labeled as recovery relative to an assumed baseline, rather than proof of truth.

## B11

Next, connect repair execution to explicit review decisions. A rejected exclusion must stay rejected, and a flag for investigation must not silently become a restoration. This preserves the human's intended boundary.

## B12

Then make small or unfamiliar input layouts explicit. Recognize the schema or explain why it cannot be assessed. That extends the useful evidence-first workflow to the difficult cases where confidence should be lower.

## B13

Keep the strengths and make the boundaries more explicit. This is a research prototype, and a useful starting point for further development. The linked independent review contains the detailed evidence and remaining limitations.

## BVDT

Let's recap. Connect changes to analysis results. Retain content fingerprints and evidence. Label repair simulations as assumptions. Make exports follow explicit decisions.

## BHTF

Help me test SNS Control Tower with invented releases. Compare a repair simulation with an approved repair. Include a rejected exclusion and a small input table. Explain the evidence and review decision required before export.
