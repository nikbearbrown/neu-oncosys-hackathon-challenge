## BDEFS

Three terms. Question card means the intended use and its required checks. Fingerprint means a digest tied to particular evidence. Sign-off means a recorded human review decision.

## B01

A question card names what matters. Checks produce evidence. A review receipt records the decision. Keeping these connected makes it easier to ask why a result was reached and what would make it change.
