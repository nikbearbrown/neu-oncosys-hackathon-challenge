# Film branding and attribution

Event organizers and sponsors: **Northeastern University · OncoSys AI · The Open Accelerator · Red Hat**.

Bear requested Northeastern SEIS credits in the films alongside the university identity. SEIS is pronounced like Spanish six. It is not SASE or SACE. All first and last beats name the project and verified team, show an animated conceptual process, and credit the sponsors. Opening narration identifies sponsorship; the closing thanks participants and sponsors.

Supplied logos are preserved in proportion and artwork. Red Hat uses Bear's original `Red_hat_logo.png`, copied byte-for-byte. OncoSys AI and The Open Accelerator use the supplied, approved transparent derivatives. SEIS retains its supplied black background; the Northeastern university vector comes from the existing branding collection. Trademarks are not relicensed by this repository.
